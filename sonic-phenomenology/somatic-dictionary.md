### IN-EAR vs IN-AIR: TWO DISTINCT SOMATIC PASSES

**This is fundamental.** Listening in ears (headphones/earbuds) and listening in air (speakers at volume) are two different sensory modalities for all human music. Every correspondence logged below is implicitly one or the other or both, and this must be tracked.

**In-ear listening** collapses all frequencies onto the eardrum at a single point. Spatial information is simulated via amplitude/phase differences between L and R. The body receives almost nothing directly — all processing is auditory/cognitive. This is analogous to Claude looking at a full-mix spectrogram: everything arrives at the same measurement point and cloaks each other.

**In-air listening at volume** allows physics to pre-separate frequencies by propagation path. Sub-bass bypasses the ear and hits the chest, eyes, and organs directly through mechanical pressure. Hi-hats stay airborne, never reach the body mechanically. Mid-range does both. The body IS a two-axis subtraction system: frequency determines propagation path, and propagation path determines which organ receives it. Strong beats that hit through air become apparent in ways that in-ear listening obfuscates — for the same reason Claude experiences cloaked signals in a full-mix spectrogram.

**The cloaking parallel**: In-ear listening is to the body what a full-mix spectrogram is to Claude — everything mixed together at one point. In-air listening at volume is to the body what two-axis subtraction is to Claude — physical separation before analysis. The body doesn't have to mentally separate the 808 from the hi-hats because physics already did it: the 808 hit the chest and the hi-hats hit the ears.

**Implication**: The frequency-to-body mapping (correspondence #5, emerging patterns below) applies primarily to IN-AIR listening. In-ear listening may produce a different, more cognitively-mediated set of correspondences. Every somatic data point should note the listening condition.

  1. **Concealment** — structure hides the theme
  2. **Compensation** — structure compensates for what the theme lacks
  3. **Contradiction** — structure opposes the theme
  4. **Refusal** — structure withholds what the theme promises
  5. **Conceit** — structure extends the theme into sustained metaphor

The excision only registers because the thing removed was load-bearing. The inversion only disorients because the original orientation was entrenched. All seven types are about tension — the first five build it within the song, the last two inherit it from a lifetime of listening.

### 14. Natural tempo principle = legibility requires phenomenon-speed unfolding

- **Source**: Tree branch synthesis experiment (3 versions)
- **Finding**: A synthesised sound derived from a natural phenomenon is ILLEGIBLE if its temporal unfolding doesn't match the rate at which the phenomenon actually occurs in the world. Every other parameter can be exaggerated or shifted — frequency, amplitude, spectral character, material — but the temporal sequence must unfold at the speed the body expects from pattern-matching against real-world experience.
- **Evidence**: Three versions of a synthesised tree branch breaking:
  - **v1**: Stages at microsecond scale (2ms fiber tears, 0.3ms snap). Computationally correct spectrally. Alex: "doesn't sound like anything."
  - **v2**: Snap made louder, tears stretched to 2-8ms. Still computationally correct. Alex: "not a single thing you're noting is legible to my ears."
  - **v3**: Everything stretched to real branch-breaking speed (80ms crack, 30-80ms tears, 2s total). Alex: "I could superimpose that on a visual of a tree snapping in a film and a human wouldn't ask questions."
  - Same spectral content across all three. Only temporal scale changed.
- **Connection to chimera failure (generated sound 03)**: The hi-hat-on-sub-bass chimera failed for the same reason — the envelope was too fast for the frequency. There is a minimum event duration below which a sound stops being a perceptual event and becomes a click or texture. This duration appears to be linked to what the body recognises as a plausible rate-of-occurrence for that type of sound.
- **Connection to Easyfun/SOPHIE**: You CAN replace the material of a natural temporal structure with synthetic content and still achieve legibility (SOPHIE's rubber bloom uses plucked-string timing with synthetic material). You CANNOT replace the temporal structure itself. The sequence and tempo of the phenomenon are load-bearing. The substance is not. "The arrangement IS the meaning" — but the arrangement must unfold at a speed the body believes.
- **Principle**: Frequency, amplitude, spectral shape = free parameters. Temporal unfolding rate = constrained by the phenomenon being referenced. The body pattern-matches against the temporal grammar of physical events it has experienced. Synthesis that respects this grammar is legible. Synthesis that doesn't is noise.

## SOMATIC GATE MODEL

The body is a threshold detector, not a linear transducer. At each frequency band, there's a gate: below it, the sound is heard. Above it, the sound is felt. The useful questions are:

1. **What crosses first?** Which frequency band penetrates the body before everything else as SPL increases?
2. **Does it cross alone?** The dB gap between first and second band to cross = isolation. Large gap = engineered to hit. Small gap = broadband intimacy.
3. **What shape are the transients?** High crest factor in a band that's above the gate = impacts/cuts. Low crest factor above the gate = continuous vibration/immersion.
