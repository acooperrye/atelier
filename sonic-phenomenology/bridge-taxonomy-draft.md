## BRIDGE TYPE TAXONOMY

Seven types. Each defined by its dimensional mechanism, detection signature, and somatic character. The taxonomy grows as more songs are analyzed — new types can be added without protocol changes.

**Canonical sources:** Types 1-5 are detailed below. Types 6-7 are defined in `somatic-dictionary.md` (where they were discovered) and summarized in `module-bridge.md`. The full detection and somatic data for Types 6-7 lives in somatic correspondences #12 (Lemonade/Excision) and #15 (Circle Pit/Inversion).

**Two axes of tension:**
- Types 1-5: Structure ↔ Theme (what the music does vs what it's about). Work inside the song. Can function on first listen.
- Types 6-7: Musical Structure ↔ Musical Semantics (conventions vs subversions). Work in the gap between the song and the listener's priors. Biographical bridges — require a lifetime of listening.

### Type Definitions

#### 1. CONCEALMENT

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Character** | Surface says one thing, architecture says another. The structure hides what the content reveals. |
| **Dimensional pattern** | Cross-dimensional. High-VALENCE or High-DENSITY structure carrying Low-VALENCE theme. Bright/clean/stable structure over dark/messy/unstable meaning. |
| **Detection** | After within-dimension scoring, compare top structural readings (DENSITY, VALENCE, STABILITY) against ALL thematic dimensions. If strong structural DENSITY contradicts strong thematic VALENCE â†’ flag CONCEALMENT. The tension crosses dimensions, not within them. |
| **Convergence verb** | *Hiding.* All layers are hiding. The compression hides. The brightness hides. The grid hides. The stereo field hides. |
| **Somatic signature** | Two simultaneous experiences that don't resolve. Body registers weight the ears deny. Comfort on the surface, unease underneath. The feeling of being lied to pleasantly. |
| **Prototype** | EWTRTW â€” cheerful synth-pop surface performing the exact mechanism its lyrics describe (power making itself invisible, comfortable, permanent). Structure IS the thing the lyrics warn about. |
| **Span** | Typically long. The concealment works precisely because the distance between surface and architecture is wide enough that the listener can miss it entirely. |

#### 2. COMPENSATION

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Character** | Structure compensates for what meaning lacks. The structure fills what the content empties. |
| **Dimensional pattern** | Cross-dimensional. High-DENSITY structure + Low-DENSITY theme (filling emptiness). Dense/loud/crowded sonic architecture carrying empty/lonely/sparse meaning. Can also cross DENSITY-structure with SCALE-theme (surrounding sound around an isolated subject). |
| **Detection** | Strong structural DENSITY readings combined with thematic content scoring negative on DENSITY or SCALE. The structure over-provides what the lyrics describe as absent. Look for thematic mixed signals â€” aggregate may read as community (+0.4) while shadow layer reads as isolation. |
| **Convergence verb** | *Filling.* All layers are filling. The polyphonic density fills. The stereo width fills. The reverb fills. The arrangement fills. |
| **Somatic signature** | Surrounded but alone. Held by sound that can't actually hold you. The fullness of the music makes the emptiness of the subject MORE visible, not less. Compensation that illuminates rather than fixes. |
| **Prototype** | NTLTC â€” 65-82 simultaneous voices singing about being alone. Maximum density around post-grief resilience. Stereo trajectory 0.02â†’0.89 = starts surrounded, ends intimate. The structure fills the space grief emptied. |
| **Span** | Medium to long. The compensation is audible (you CAN hear the fullness) but the gap between what the sound provides and what the subject needs persists. |

#### 3. CONTRADICTION

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Character** | Direct opposition between structure and meaning. Neither wins. The tension IS the content. |
| **Dimensional pattern** | Within-dimensional. Structure and theme score opposite on the SAME dimension. Both readings are strong; neither overrides the other. The opposition is the point. |
| **Detection** | Within-dimension scoring produces strong negative sign where BOTH structural and thematic readings are high-magnitude. Unlike REFUSAL (where structure wins) or CONCEALMENT (where structure hides), CONTRADICTION holds both in suspension. Neither layer dominates. |
| **Convergence verb** | *Questioning.* All layers are questioning. The machine-precision questions. The existential lyrics question. The synth timbres question. The ontological instability questions. |
| **Somatic signature** | Vertigo. Genuine uncertainty about what is real. Not discomfort from being lied to (CONCEALMENT) or from absence being surrounded (COMPENSATION), but from the ground being genuinely unstable. The song doesn't know what it is, and that unknowing IS the experience. |
| **Prototype** | BG â€” machine performing feeling. Structure reads AGENCY as mechanical, theme reads AGENCY as desperately human. Structure reads STABILITY as ontologically unstable, theme asks "am I real?" Neither layer resolves the question. The contradiction is the song's thesis. |
| **Span** | Short. The two sides of the tension are close enough to see simultaneously. The listener doesn't have to dig to find the opposition â€” it's on the surface, and the closeness is what creates the vertigo. |

#### 4. REFUSAL

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Character** | Structure refuses to perform what meaning requests. The structure says "no." |
| **Dimensional pattern** | Within-dimensional, but asymmetric. Strong structural reading and strong thematic score in opposite direction on the SAME dimension, where structure wins. The content escalates; the structure stays flat. The content asks for drama; the structure withholds it. |
| **Detection** | Same within-dimension opposition as CONTRADICTION, but with a dominance marker: structural readings remain static or narrow-range despite thematic content that would typically demand structural response. Look for flat dynamics despite dramatic lyrics. Low energy structure despite high energy theme. The structure's refusal to move is the finding. |
| **Convergence verb** | *Withholding.* All layers are withholding. The dynamics withhold. The arrangement withholds. The stereo field withholds. The structure knows what the content wants and won't give it. |
| **Somatic signature** | Frustration that resolves into recognition. The body wants the music to DO something in response to the lyrics, and the music won't. Initial response is "why isn't this building?" â€” mature response is "oh, the refusal IS the statement." The restraint communicates more than compliance would. |
| **Prototype** | EWTRTW dynamic range â€” the most dramatic lyrics ("wars," "surveillance," "impermanence") in the flattest landscape. The structure refuses to darken, refuses to escalate, refuses to match the gravity of the content. The refusal performs the lyric's thesis: power doesn't need to raise its voice. |
| **Span** | Medium. The gap is perceptible (you notice the flatness against the drama) but the structure's dominance means one side of the bridge is anchored. Less destabilizing than CONTRADICTION, more deliberate than CONCEALMENT. |

#### 5. CONCEIT

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Character** | Structure and meaning agree â€” but through indirect means. The agreement is metaphorical, not literal. The structure doesn't depict the theme; it ENACTS an analogous process. |
| **Dimensional pattern** | Cross-dimensional agreement through structural metaphor. Structure and theme may appear to diverge on individual dimensions, but the PROCESS the structure performs mirrors the PROCESS the theme describes. Not sign-matching on a vector â€” pattern-matching on a verb. |
| **Detection** | Standard dimensional comparison may read as neutral or mildly reinforced. The conceit reveals itself in the convergence verb test: when you ask "what is every layer DOING?", the answer is a single verb that describes both the structural process and the thematic process, but the structural version is non-obvious. Detection requires the somatic inference step â€” the mechanism won't flag it automatically. |
| **Convergence verb** | *[Variable â€” the verb IS the conceit].* The defining feature: the convergence verb describes a process shared by structure and meaning through analogy, not identity. Structure doesn't sound like what the lyrics say â€” structure BEHAVES like what the lyrics mean. |
| **Somatic signature** | Recognition. Not tension, not vertigo, not frustration â€” the "oh" moment when you realize the structure has been doing what the lyrics mean all along, just in a different language. The body gets it before the mind names it. A click rather than a pull. |
| **Prototype** | No confirmed prototype. Theoretical candidates: songs where structural behavior (e.g., a harmonic pattern that "searches" while lyrics describe searching, without the harmony being conventionally "searching-sounding") creates meaning through process analogy rather than surface correspondence. |
| **Span** | Variable â€” potentially zero. If the conceit is tight, structure and meaning occupy the same space through different means. The "span" is linguistic, not experiential. You only see the gap if you describe the two layers separately; if you describe what the song DOES, the gap disappears. |

#### 6. EXCISION

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Character** | A semantic layer is removed entirely. Meaning persists through arrangement alone. The bridge is recognising that meaning survived the surgery. |
| **Dimensional pattern** | Not dimensional in the standard sense. The absence of an expected layer (typically vocals-as-address, or conventional song structure) creates a gap that arrangement fills. Detection requires knowing what SHOULD be there — the Cultural Engine's convention bank enumerates expected elements per genre. |
| **Detection** | Harder than any other type. Inversion (Type 7) is presence of something unexpected — measurable. Excision is absence of something expected. The system needs to enumerate what the genre baseline predicts and then confirm that a predicted element is genuinely absent, not just quiet. Convention violation with `violation_signature: { expected_present: true, actually_absent: true }`. Open question: is excision primarily somatic (body feels the absence) or cognitive (mind notices it)? |
| **Convergence verb** | *Persisting.* Meaning persists despite removal. The arrangement persists. The listener's pattern-matching persists. The absence is not silence — it is the shape of what was removed, still visible in the negative space. |
| **Somatic signature** | Recognition that something is missing, followed by recognition that it doesn't matter — meaning survived. The body doesn't feel absence directly; it feels the rearrangement of what remains to fill the gap. Like furniture shifted to cover where something stood. |
| **Prototype** | SOPHIE — Lemonade. The semantic layer (conventional vocal address, narrative, human vulnerability) is excised. What remains: synthetic rubber bubbles, pitch-shifted voice-as-material, arrangement-as-meaning. "In the absence of an authorial voice, what does the arrangement mean?" Meaning persists because arrangement is an irreducibly human act. |
| **Span** | Variable. Depends on how load-bearing the excised element was. If the removed layer was central to the genre's semantic contract (e.g., vocals in pop), the span is wide. |
| **Biographical prerequisite** | The listener must have internalized the convention being violated. If you've never expected vocals-as-address in electronic music, the excision doesn't register. The bridge requires a lifetime of "songs have singers who mean things" to feel the absence. |

#### 7. INVERSION

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Character** | Structural roles swap. What was rhythmic becomes tonal. What was felt becomes heard. What was external becomes internal. The listener's perceptual frame must flip to accommodate. The bridge is the surrender to reorientation. |
| **Dimensional pattern** | Cross-role. Convention violations where elements perform the opposite of their expected function: percussion reads as harmonic (HPSS inversion), sub-bass provides the only legible rhythm (role inversion), the listener's body claims the music as internal signal (attribution inversion). Multiple simultaneous inversions compound. |
| **Detection** | More tractable than Excision. Convention violations are measurable as the presence of unexpected configurations. The Cultural Engine's fingerprint validation pass can flag: HPSS ratio inverted (percussion reads harmonic), onset ratio inverted (sub-bass onset ratio near 1.0 instead of >4.0), spectral role inversion (sub-bass as metronome, breaks as drone). Three violations in Circle Pit all signal Type 7. |
| **Convergence verb** | *Flipping.* All layers are flipping. The percussion flips to drone. The sub-bass flips to beat. The external music flips to internal circulation. The listener's frame flips from resistance to surrender. |
| **Somatic signature** | Two phases. (1) Fever dream / internal misattribution: sub-bass pulse rate is near-physiological, body claims it as internal signal (heartbeat, blood pressure). Disorientation. (2) Rollercoaster / intentioned intensity: cognition clicks back, reframes sensation as external and intentional. Intensity doesn't change. Framing does. The bridge is the moment you stop fighting the disorientation and join the rotation. |
| **Prototype** | Venetian Snares — Circle Pit. Amen snares at ~5.7 hits/sec fuse into continuous tonal excitation (HPSS 70.8% harmonic). Sub-bass shadow operates as only legible beat (onset ratio 1.1x). You hear the drone and feel the beat. Opposite of everything. |
| **Span** | Short to medium. The inversion is immediate and total — roles don't gradually shift, they swap. The span is the distance between the listener's prior orientation and the new one. |
| **Biographical prerequisite** | The listener must have deeply internalized what drums "should" do (be rhythmic), what sub-bass "should" do (be felt not counted), and what music "should" be (external). Circle Pit inverts all three simultaneously. Without those priors, it's just noise with bass. With them, it's vertigo. |

### Distinguishing CONTRADICTION from REFUSAL

Both are within-dimensional tensions. The diagnostic:

| | CONTRADICTION | REFUSAL |
|---|---|---|
| **Power balance** | Neither side wins | Structure wins |
| **Structural behavior** | Structure actively participates in the opposition | Structure remains static while content moves |
| **Somatic feel** | Vertigo (ground unstable) | Frustration â†’ recognition (ground solid, but won't move) |
| **Verb** | Questioning | Withholding |
| **The tension is...** | The content itself | A commentary on the content |

### Distinguishing CONCEALMENT from COMPENSATION

Both are cross-dimensional tensions. The diagnostic:

| | CONCEALMENT | COMPENSATION |
|---|---|---|
| **Structural intent** | Structure hides the theme | Structure fills what the theme empties |
| **Primary dimension crossing** | VALENCE-structure â†” VALENCE-theme (bright hiding dark) | DENSITY-structure â†” DENSITY-theme (full surrounding empty) |
| **Somatic feel** | Two simultaneous truths (surface comfort + deep unease) | Surrounded but alone (presence highlighting absence) |
| **Verb** | Hiding | Filling |
| **What the listener discovers** | "There's something underneath" | "The fullness makes the emptiness louder" |

### Distinguishing EXCISION from INVERSION

Both are biographical bridges (Types 6-7). The diagnostic:

| | EXCISION | INVERSION |
|---|---|---|
| **What changes** | Something is removed | Roles are swapped |
| **Detection method** | Absence of expected element | Presence of unexpected configuration |
| **Computational tractability** | Hard (absence detection) | Easier (convention violation detection) |
| **Somatic feel** | Recognition that meaning survived removal | Disorientation from role swap → surrender |
| **Verb** | Persisting | Flipping |
| **The tension is...** | Between what's missing and what remains | Between the listener's priors and the music's actual structure |
| **Cultural Engine signal** | Expected element absent from binary data | Expected relationship inverted in binary data |

### Conceit as the Limit Case

CONCEIT is what happens when the bridge span approaches zero through metaphor rather than through literal agreement. It's distinct from "no bridge tension" (which would be a jingle â€” structure and meaning directly aligned). In CONCEIT, the alignment is real but indirect. The structure doesn't sound like the meaning; it works like the meaning.

This makes CONCEIT the hardest type to detect computationally and the most rewarding to discover somatically. It's the type most likely to emerge from the human side of the analysis rather than the computational side.

### Type Distribution Expectations (Theoretical)

| Type | Expected frequency | Why |
|------|-------------------|-----|
| CONCEALMENT | Common in pop, synth-pop, R&B | Commercial genres reward pleasant surfaces over matching surfaces |
| COMPENSATION | Common in maximalist pop, orchestral, shoegaze | Dense production is available and frequently deployed around vulnerable content |
| CONTRADICTION | Common in experimental, art rock, electronic | Genres that tolerate or celebrate unresolved tension |
| REFUSAL | Common in post-punk, ambient, minimalist | Genres with aesthetic commitments to restraint |
| CONCEIT | Genre-independent, rare | Depends on compositional sophistication, not genre convention |
| EXCISION | Rare — electronic, hyperpop, noise | Requires genre where semantic removal is possible without collapse. Genres with strong non-vocal traditions. |
| INVERSION | Rare — breakcore, noise, experimental electronic | Requires genre where convention violations are extreme enough to swap structural roles entirely. |

Types 6-7 are inherently rarer because they require both extreme production choices AND listeners with deep enough priors to register the violation. A first-time listener to breakcore cannot experience Inversion as a bridge — it's just unfamiliar music. The biographical prerequisite is a filter on the audience, not the music.

These are hypotheses. The dictionary will confirm or revise as entries accumulate.
